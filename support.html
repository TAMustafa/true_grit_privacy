Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I play Sudoku?

A: Fill the 9×9 grid so that each row, column, and 3×3 box contains the digits 1-9 without repetition. Tap a cell to select it, then tap a number to fill it in.

Q: How do I use notes mode?

A: Tap the pencil icon to enable notes mode. In this mode, you can add small numbers to cells as reminders of possible values. Tap the pencil again to return to normal mode.

Q: How do I use hints?

A: Tap the lightbulb icon to reveal a correct number in the selected cell. Use hints sparingly as they affect your score!

Q: How do I change the difficulty level?

A: Tap the settings icon (gear) in the top right corner, then select your preferred difficulty level: Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert.

Q: How do I switch between light and dark mode?

A: Go to Settings and tap the theme toggle. Pure Grid supports both light and dark modes to match your preference.

Q: Where are my statistics stored?

A: All your game statistics are stored locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers. Your privacy is our priority.

Q: Can I play offline?

A: Yes! Pure Grid works completely offline. No internet connection is required to play or access your statistics.

Q: How do I clear my statistics?

A: Go to Settings → Statistics → Clear All Statistics. This will permanently delete all your game history. You can also delete the app to remove all data.

Q: What happens if I make a mistake?

A: If auto-check is enabled (Settings), incorrect numbers will be highlighted in red. You can tap the cell again and choose a different number. Mistakes are tracked in your game statistics.

Q: Can I pause a game?

A: Yes! Tap the pause button or simply close the app. Your game progress is automatically saved, and you can resume anytime.

Q: How is my score calculated?

A: Your score is based on completion time, number of mistakes, and hints used. Faster times with fewer mistakes and no hints result in higher scores.

Q: Does Pure Grid collect my personal data?

A: No. Pure Grid does not collect any personal data. All game information stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Q: Is Pure Grid suitable for children?

A: Yes! Pure Grid is safe for all ages. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no data collection.

Q: Can I generate unlimited puzzles?

A: Yes! Tap "New Game" to generate a fresh puzzle anytime. Each puzzle is randomly generated and unique.

Troubleshooting

App crashes or freezes

  1. Close the app completely and reopen it
  2. Restart your device
  3. Ensure you have the latest version of Pure Grid from the App Store
  4. If the issue persists, contact support

Statistics not saving

  1. Ensure you complete the puzzle (fill all cells correctly)
  2. Check that you have sufficient storage space on your device
  3. Try clearing and restarting the app

Sound effects not playing

  1. Check that your device is not in silent mode
  2. Verify that sound effects are enabled in Settings
  3. Check your device's volume settings

Theme not changing

  1. Go to Settings and toggle the theme switch
  2. Close and reopen the app
  3. If using system theme, check your device's appearance settings
